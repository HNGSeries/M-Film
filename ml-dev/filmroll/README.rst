Film roll
=========

Shoot like a film camera: pick a film stock, load a roll and shoot a fixed
number of frames. Loading a roll applies picture style, white balance and
ISO for that stock and writes a roll log (ROLL_nnn.TXT) next to the images,
which the PC film lab develops.

:Summary: Film stocks, rolls and a roll log for the PC film lab
